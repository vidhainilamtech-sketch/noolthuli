Good to know

Customised saree FAQs

Can I order just one customised saree?

Yes. For personal gifts like proposals, couple names, birthdays and anniversaries you can order even a single saree.

Can you print Tamil or Malayalam text?

Yes. Names and messages can be in English, Tamil or Malayalam. Send the exact words on WhatsApp and we will share a preview.

Print or embroidery – which should I choose?

Printing is great for names, photos and detailed art at a lower price. Embroidery gives a rich, raised thread look for names, motifs and logos.

How long does a customised saree take?

Usually 7 to 15 days after you approve the design. For birthdays, weddings and festivals please order early.

Do you take bulk orders for shops?

Yes. We supply textile shops, boutiques, colleges, offices and families in bulk with trade pricing. Message us on WhatsApp with quantity and design.
