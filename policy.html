Shipping, Payment & Privacy

Simple, honest policies.

Shipping

  • FREE shipping on all orders across India.
  • Orders are packed carefully and dispatched after confirmation on WhatsApp.
  • Tracking details, where available, are shared on WhatsApp.

No refund, no return

  • Once an item is purchased there is NO refund and NO return.
  • Please confirm colour, design and price with us on WhatsApp before you pay.
  • If you receive a wrong item, message us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery with an unboxing video.

Payment

  • Payment is accepted only via GPay / UPI. No cash on delivery and no card payments.
  • Paying online at checkout is optional — you can confirm your order first and we will share payment details on WhatsApp.
  • Always share your payment screenshot in the WhatsApp chat so we can confirm quickly.

Privacy

  • No data is stored on any server. This website has no database.
  • Your cart is saved only in your own browser (localStorage) and is cleared after you place an order.
  • Your name, phone and address are sent only to us, by you, through WhatsApp.
  • Your payment screenshot is never uploaded — it is used only in your browser to make your PDF receipt.
  • Product photos and fonts are loaded from trusted third-party services (Unsplash, Google Fonts, cdnjs).
