Our story

A drop of thread, a lifetime of grace

Noolthuli (நூல்துளி — “a drop of thread”) is a saree manufacturer devoted to the comfort and beauty of pure cotton sarees. Founded by Jairam, we print and embroider every saree in our own unit, and customise colours, prints and embroidery to your choice, with patience and pride.

Because we make what we sell, you get honest pricing, careful quality checks and a saree that feels as special as the day you wear it.
